Rs is estimated as 1.18 × ΔtR / (FWHM₁ + FWHM₂). Undefined widths or resolution are shown as —.

Method: three-point smoothing, prominence screening, valley-based peak segmentation, local linear baseline, and trapezoidal integration. These estimates do not perform Gaussian/EMG deconvolution, identify compounds, validate a method, or establish spectral peak purity or USP conformity. Review against reference standards and qualified chromatography software for regulated applications.
